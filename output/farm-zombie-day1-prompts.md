# Day 1: Surviving a Zombie Apocalypse on a Farm — Production Prompts

9:16 · 100 seconds · 10 clips of about 10 seconds · motion comic stickman · locked to your character, dusk and night reference images.

## How to use

- Paste each prompt into your video generator as one block. Every prompt is self-contained.
- Attach the reference images listed for each clip if your tool supports reference images (Image 1 = man and girl, Image 2 = dusk farm, Image 3 = night farm).
- Generate clip 1 first and check the characters and voice before generating the rest.

## Global continuity (review summary)

- **Characters:** the man (black parka, messy brown hair) and the girl (long blonde hair, beige bow, cream dress), as in Image 1. The zombie appears from clip 8 onwards and is never gory.
- **Locations:** clips 1–2 dusk farm (Image 2), clips 3–7 farmhouse interior, clips 8–10 night farm (Image 3). Farmhouse windows are dark with no smoke in clip 1 and light up at the end of clip 2.
- **Narrator:** one off-screen male narrator; the full voice block is repeated word for word in every prompt. He also reads "What's that?".
- **Music:** a low synth drone with sparse plucked guitar from clip 1 to the end. A heartbeat pulse is added from clip 7, the music drops almost to silence in clip 8, and it ends on an unresolved chord.

## Voice and music check

1. Every prompt contains the identical narrator block. If clips sound like different people, first check nothing in a prompt was edited, then regenerate only that clip.
2. Listen to the clips back to back. Prompt text can't guarantee the generator will produce the same voice every time.
3. If voices still don't match, use one continuous voiceover recorded or generated separately and lay it over the edit.

## Known risks to check

- **Character consistency:** check hair, the bow clip, the parka and head size in every clip, especially when the man's arms come out of his pockets (clips 3, 5, 6, 7, 9).
- **Interiors:** clips 3–7 have no reference image, so rooms may vary between clips. Making kitchen, cellar and living-room reference images first is the best fix.
- **Counts:** the generator may not draw exactly 16 jugs; the on-screen number comes from the overlay.
- **Lettering:** watch for stray letters on cans, the notebook or comic sound words, and regenerate any clip that has them.
