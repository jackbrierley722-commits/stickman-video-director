# They Hunt Rhythm — Director's Proposal (Phase A)

**Mode:** A · Director (A1.2 Classic Black & White, white on black)
**Platform:** TikTok · `9:16` · `60s` · 6 parts × ~10s (one Omni Flash clip per part)

> Setup choices made for this request: 9:16 because it's for TikTok, 60s / 6 clips because a 6-part video was requested, and A1.2 (white on black) because a horror story reads well on a pitch-black canvas. Any of these can be changed. A global change means rewriting this proposal.

---

## 1. Title

**They Hunt Rhythm**
(The guide and the voiceover are both in English, so no reference translation is needed.)

## 2. Core message & hook

- **The key thing about the aliens:** they are **blind, and they don't hunt noise, they hunt *rhythm***. Footsteps, ticking clocks and heartbeats bring them straight to you. Random, messy noise makes you invisible to them.
- **Golden hook (0–3s):** *"They have no eyes. They can't see you. So why did every silent hiding place go dark first?"* The obvious answer, "stay quiet", turns out to be the deadly one.
- **The final decision (two options, Part 6):** the only way to lure the swarm into a flooded quarry is for someone to beat a drum by hand, steadily, until the aliens arrive.
  - **A · Drum.** You stay. Two hundred survivors live. Your little brother grows up without you.
  - **B · Run.** You take your brother and run off-beat into the chaos. You both live. The tower goes silent and the swarm finds the shelter.

## 4. Narrator voice profile (for the whole video)

Adult female, General American accent, low warm alto, medium-low pitch, soft chest resonance, very slight natural breathiness, no rasp. Hushed, intimate, controlled delivery, like telling the viewer a secret. Restrained pitch variation, steady unhurried pace, close and dry studio sound. Second-person narration ("you"), so the viewer *is* the survivor.

- English VO: **134 words**, about 55–60s at a steady pace
- This profile has not been tested on a provider. Only the male block in `voice-continuity.md` has listening acceptance. Listen to the clips side by side after generating them.

## 5. Accent colours (three at most, each with one job)

| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Acid green** | The invaders: their glowing line bodies and their ships |
| **Vivid red** | Rhythm, the thing they track: heartbeat pulse rings, clock ticks, footstep rings, drum rings |
| **Warm gold** | Human hope: lit windows, the radio-tower beacon, the glow between two held hands |

Everything else is white line art on black. The stick figures stay white; colour only appears on props, creatures and graphic effects.

## 6. Cast (frozen for every clip)

- **You:** the adult stick figure (Style 1 Dark anchor).
- **Little brother:** the same line rules, about two-thirds of your height. Appears in Parts 5–6.
- **Survivors:** small white stick figures in groups (tower crew, shelter crowd).
- **The invaders:** tall, thin, three-legged creatures drawn as acid-green glowing line art. Smooth eyeless oval heads, long hanging arms. They tilt their heads to *listen*. They never have eyes.

## 7. BGM, tone and arc

- **BGM:** a low, sustained dark synth drone with a faint, slow, *irregular* ticking pulse. The same instruments and tempo from Part 1 to Part 6. Tension comes from SFX layering, not from changing the music.
- **Key SFX motif:** a soft, steady **heartbeat / drum thump** is the sound of danger. Clattering, uneven **tin-can rattles** are the sound of safety.
- **Tone:** quiet survival horror → clever discovery → hope → impossible choice.
- **Arc (5-stage engine):** Hook (P1) → Disrupting Assumptions: silence kills (P2) → Insider Secret: they hunt rhythm, chaos hides you (P3) → Mechanism → Truth: one giant rhythm could trap them all, but it costs a person (P4–P5) → Elevation & comment debate: A or B? (P6)
