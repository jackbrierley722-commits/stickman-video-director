# They Hunt Rhythm — Series Director's Proposal (Phase A)

**Mode:** A · Director (A1.2 Classic Black & White, white on black)
**Platform:** TikTok · `9:16` · **6 standalone episodes × ~45s**

> **How 45 seconds is built:** the video generator makes clips of about 10 seconds, and this repo's contract works in 10-second blocks. Each episode is **5 clips**. Every clip is generated at ~10s and trimmed to its best **~9s** in the edit (5 × 9s ≈ 45s). Narration per clip is kept to about 15–25 words so it fits inside 9 seconds.

## Series premise

- **The key thing about the aliens:** they're **blind, and they don't hunt noise, they hunt rhythm**. Footsteps, ticking clocks and heartbeats lead them to you. Random, messy noise makes you invisible.
- **You** (second-person narration) and your **little brother** survive by learning that rule, one episode at a time.
- Every episode is a full 5-stage arc of its own: Hook → Twist → Secret → Truth → Cliffhanger. Episodes 1–5 end on a cliffhanger, and Episode 6 ends on the **A/B decision**:
  - **A · Drum.** You stay on the tower. Two hundred survivors live. Your brother grows up without you.
  - **B · Run.** You take his hand and run off-beat. You both live. The tower goes silent and the tunnels go dark.

## Global settings (shared by all 6 episodes)

| Setting | Choice |
|---|---|
| Ratio | `9:16` vertical |
| Length | ~45s per episode (5 clips × ~9s after trim) |
| Style | Style 1 Classic Minimalist, Dark: flat pitch-black canvas, pure white line art, occasional simple line expressions |
| Accent colours | **Acid green** = invaders · **vivid red** = rhythm (the thing they hunt) · **warm gold** = human hope |
| Narrator | One adult female for the whole series: General American, low warm alto, hushed and intimate, steady pace, close dry studio sound. Untested on a provider, so compare clips by listening. |
| BGM | One low dark synth drone with a faint *irregular* ticking pulse, the same in every episode so the series sounds like one show |
| SFX motif | A steady heartbeat or drum = danger; messy tin-can clatter = safety |

**Cast (frozen):** the adult stick figure (you); the little brother, same line rules at ⅔ height; survivors as small white stick figures; the invaders, tall, thin, three-legged acid-green glowing line creatures with smooth **eyeless** oval heads and long hanging arms, who tilt their heads to listen.

**Series continuity:** each episode's first frame recreates the previous episode's last frame, so they play back-to-back on a TikTok playlist.
