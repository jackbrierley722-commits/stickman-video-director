# They Hunt Rhythm — Series Plan (3 × 1-minute episodes)

TikTok · 9:16 · **3 episodes**, each **8 clips of ~7.5s** made in Google Flow (~1 minute).

## The story

Blind aliens hunt **rhythm**, not noise: heartbeats, ticking clocks, humming. Messy random noise keeps you invisible. You protect your little sister, who thinks it's all a game. It ends on a choice: **A · drum** and save 200 people but leave her alone, or **B · run** with her and let the tunnels go dark.

| Ep | Title | Ends on |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | They Can't See | Long black fingers slide under the door |
| 2 | They Hunt Rhythm | 200 people are hiding below a tower, and they need you |
| 3 | Drum or Run? | A or B? Comment what you'd do |

## Look

- **Style:** eerie flat 2D cartoon with cold grey-navy colours, fog and moonlight.
- **The man (you):** white round head, short messy black hair, dark grey hoodie.
- **His little sister:** pigtails, a yellow raincoat and a bunny. She doesn't understand the danger.
- **The aliens:** tall, thin, pitch-black, faceless and inhuman.
- **Narrator:** a deep whispering male voice.

Subtitles and the header are added in CapCut, not Flow, because AI video misspells words. The monitors contain no numbers or letters, so Flow can draw them reliably.

## Narration (all 3 episodes)

**Episode 1 · They Can't See**

1. At 11:52 PM, the power dies. Something tall walks past. It has no eyes. That's the worst part.
2. You wave right in front of the glass. It doesn't react. It can't see. You think you're safe.
3. Your little sister wakes, thinking it's a game. You whisper the rules: silent, still. You hide under the table.
4. Across the street, a barking dog's house stays lit. But the silent houses go dark, one by one.
5. The quiet gets so deep you hear it. Thump. Thump. Your heartbeat, pulsing through the wall.
6. Outside, something stops walking. Its head turns toward your window. And beside you, your sister starts to hum.
7. It's at the window. It can't see you. But it hears her humming, and sways in time.
8. Every instinct says freeze. But stillness makes every sound clearer. Then long black fingers slide under the door.

**Episode 2 · They Hunt Rhythm**

1. Long black fingers reach for you. Your elbow knocks the shelf. Pots crash, and the thing jerks back, lost.
2. So you keep throwing things, scoop her up, and run. Down the stairs. Out the door.
3. Day nine. You test it. Shattering glass? They ignore it. A ticking clock? One crushes it in seconds.
4. They hunt rhythm, not noise. Then her pocket starts to play. A music box, perfectly in time.
5. Three of them turn toward the music. You grab it and throw it as far as you can.
6. They follow it into a flooded subway, and never come back up. Rhythm isn't just danger. It's bait.
7. From then on, you move off-beat. Stumble. Pause. Skip. Rattle the cans. She thinks it's the best game ever.
8. Day twenty. A radio crackles. Two hundred people hide in tunnels below a tower. And they need you.

**Episode 3 · Drum or Run?**

1. Day twenty. The plan: speakers on the tower, one giant beat, and they all walk into the flooded quarry.
2. Then the generator dies. No power. No speakers. Just an old drum, and someone has to play it.
3. The drum works. But whoever plays it stays here until they arrive. There's no time to run.
4. No one else is up here. Just you, and a girl who thinks it's a game.
5. Below you, two hundred people are hiding in the dark. Without the drum, they're found before sunrise.
6. Option A: you stay and drum. Two hundred people live, but she grows up without you.
7. Option B: you take her hand and run. You both live. But the tunnels go dark.
8. The first beat is waiting. She's smiling. She doesn't understand. A or B? Tell me what you'd do.
